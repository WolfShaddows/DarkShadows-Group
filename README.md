## About Us

**DarkShadows Group** is an independent research, engineering and cybersecurity initiative.

We explore complex technical challenges through experimentation, software development, computational analysis and reproducible research.

Our work brings together different disciplines, with a focus on building, testing and documenting ideas rather than simply discussing them.

## Research

Our research activities include:

- Quantum computing and quantum information.
- Computational physics and scientific simulations.
- Artificial intelligence and local model experimentation.
- Scientific data analysis and computational methods.

## Engineering

We develop and experiment with:

- Systems programming and low-level architectures.
- Polyglot software engineering.
- Edge computing and embedded systems.
- Distributed systems and performance optimization.
- Hybrid architectures integrating legacy and modern technologies.

## Cybersecurity

Our security research focuses on:

- Defensive security engineering.
- Threat analysis and technical investigations.
- Network monitoring and anomaly detection.
- Zero-Trust architectures.
- Security validation and reproducible testing.
